Straight answers before you ask

Does it send emails for me?
No. You copy each email and send it from your own inbox, so you stay in control of your name and your domain.
Where do the prospects come from?
In early access, you add the companies you want to reach. Bring a short list of businesses you'd love to work with.
Will it make things up?
It's built to work only from the material you give it. If there isn't enough for a real finding, it tells you what's missing instead of guessing. You still check every finding against their live site before you send.
Does it guarantee new clients?
No. It helps you start better conversations with the right people. Running the call and winning the work is still yours.
What do I need to get started?
Two or three emails you've written, a clear idea of who you serve, and about ten minutes for setup.
Is this just another AI email writer?
The emails are the small part. The work is in choosing the right asset for what you sell, and finding something true and useful to say to each prospect.
