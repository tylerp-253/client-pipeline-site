Set it up once. Then every prospect gets a finding and three emails.

    Tell it what you do

    Get your value asset

    The app recommends the one asset that shows off what you sell, and tells you what to look for in each prospect.

Straight answers before you ask.

Does it send the emails for me?
No. You copy each email and send it from your own inbox, so your name and your domain stay in your hands.
Does it find prospects for me?
No. You bring the list. Add the companies you'd love to work with, paste in their material, and the app takes it from there.
Does it go read their website?
No. It works from what you paste in: homepage text, ads, or a welcome email. That's also why every finding points back to words you can see.
Does it guarantee new clients?
No. It helps you start better conversations with the right people. Running the call and winning the work is still yours, and you're good at that part.
